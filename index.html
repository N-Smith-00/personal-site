About Me

Hi, I'm Nicholas, a Computer Science student at Toronto Metropolitan University with a passion for software development and building things that solve real problems.

I enjoy working across different areas of software, from web development and backend systems to game development and computer graphics. Through my coursework and personal projects, I've worked with technologies including Python, JavaScript, C#, SQL, FastAPI, Flask, Unity, and OpenGL.

I'm particularly interested in projects where I can take an idea from concept to a working application. Whether I'm building a web tool, experimenting with graphics programming, or developing a larger software system, I enjoy learning new technologies and figuring out how the pieces fit together.

Outside of programming, I'm a varsity baseball athlete at Toronto Metropolitan University, where I maintain a high level of competitive play while balancing the demands of academics and athletics. I also work as a youth baseball coach, helping players develop their skills while building my own abilities in communication, leadership, and mentorship. These experiences have taught me the value of discipline, teamwork, and continuously working to improve.

I'm always looking for opportunities to learn, build, and take on new challenges.
